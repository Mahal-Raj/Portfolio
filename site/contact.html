02 / GOOD PLACES TO START

You do not need
the perfect opening.

A little context is enough. I would rather have a clear, human message than a polished pitch.

01

An opportunity

Tell me about the team, the systems it supports and what you want this person to learn or own.

02

A technical conversation

Cloud, networking, automation, Linux or a system you are trying to understand—I am always interested in a thoughtful problem.

03

A collaboration

Share what you are building, where the work stands and the part where another perspective would help.

04

A match

Table tennis, pickleball or chess. If that is the reason, the contact form already knows what to say.

03 / A USEFUL NOTE

What helps me
understand quickly.

  1. 01

    Why you're reaching out

    Opportunity, idea, collaboration, question or match.

  2. 02

    The important context

    What exists already, what matters and where the uncertainty is.

  3. 03

    A useful next step

    A call, a reply, a repository to read or a time to play.
