Notes about DevOps, Linux, Git, cloud and learning. Writing turns a half-understood idea into something precise enough to share.

04 / WHAT COMES BACK TO THE WORK

Different interests.
Useful ways of thinking.

I do not need every interest to become productive. Still, each one changes how I pay attention.

01

Writing gives me precision.

If I cannot explain an idea clearly, I probably do not understand it deeply enough yet.

02

Painting gives me patience.

It slows the pace down and makes small details feel worth noticing.

03

Sport gives me presence.

A rally has no room for a dozen tabs open in my head. I have to be in the point.

04

Chess gives me perspective.

It rewards looking beyond the immediate move without pretending every outcome can be controlled.

Opportunity, technical conversation or a match—there is more than one good reason to say hello.
